# EstateVoice AI — Client Demo One-Pager

## What we are demonstrating
An AI voice assistant concept for commercial real-estate lead qualification and sales follow-up. It can ask prospects what type of property they need, preferred location, approximate area, budget range, intended use, and whether they want a callback or site visit.

## Demo flow
1. Add a clearly fictional commercial lead to the EstateVoice dashboard.
2. Show consent status and the commercial qualification fields.
3. Run the dashboard's simulated conversation and review the generated summary.
4. Show the callback/site-visit follow-up queue and explain that a request is not a confirmed booking until the sales team confirms it.
5. If and only if the live test has passed, demonstrate one real AI call to a consenting test phone using the approved builder identity and verified project facts.

## What to say to the builder
“EstateVoice AI is being prepared as a commercial-property lead qualification and follow-up assistant. It is designed to collect the prospect's requirements consistently, summarize the conversation, and route callback or site-visit requests to the sales team. We will configure it with your approved project facts, lead permissions, and handoff process before a production pilot.”

## Honest status statement
The dashboard is a prototype. A Sarvam outbound campaign was created, but the last shared analytics screenshot showed one failed attempt and zero connected calls. Live calling is not yet demonstrated as working end-to-end. Until a single internal test succeeds, show the dashboard simulation and do not claim successful live dialing or automatic CRM integration.

## Do not promise yet
- Fully autonomous production calling
- Guaranteed lead conversion or sales
- CRM/WhatsApp integration unless tested end-to-end
- Automatic confirmed site visits or callbacks
- Any unverified project prices, inventory, approvals, RERA details, or possession dates
- Free real calls; provider and telephony charges may apply

## Go-live gate
A pilot may be shown as live only after: (1) Exotel Voicebot route verified, (2) one consenting internal call connects, (3) call status is visible in provider logs, (4) agent uses approved project facts, (5) opt-out and consent handling are tested, and (6) applicable charges and call windows are approved.
